Technology

Dexterous manipulation is the last unsolved frontier in robotics — and it cannot be solved with vision alone. Our tactile sensing technology closes that gap.

High-Resolution Tactile Sensing

Multi-channel tactile sensor arrays that capture contact pressure distribution in real time, designed for integration into robotic fingertips and grippers.

Robot Hand Integration

Sensor modules engineered for real robotic hands — compact, robust, and built to survive repeated contact in real-world manipulation tasks.

Touch-Driven Intelligence

Tactile data pipelines for machine learning, enabling slip detection, force control, and contact-rich manipulation policies.

About Hi Touch

Hi Touch Inc. is a robotics startup building tactile sensing solutions for next-generation robotic hands. Founded by researchers in tactile sensing and robotic manipulation, we build on years of research experience to bring human-level touch to real-world robots.

Our mission is simple: robots that can truly feel can truly help.
